## 1. Scenario

**Question:** Compare who controls requirements and product changes in generic versus custom software, and explain the consequences for users. The comparison uses Unreal Engine (generic) and PlatinumEngine (custom) in the context of action-game development.

**Users:** Game developers — programmers, artists, level designers; indirectly players who receive the final product (fictional: studio "ToRa" — fictional, 50 employees).

**Problem:** A mid-sized studio decides whether to use a ready-made engine (Unreal) or build its own. The choice affects who controls engine features, how fast new ideas can be added, and what constraints the team faces.

**Constraints:** 
1. Limited budget: building a custom engine requires 6–7 programmers and about 2 years before the first release (fictional: studio budget — $5M).
2. Project deadline is 3 years; if the engine is not ready on time, the game will miss its release (fictional).

**Risk:**  If the studio chooses a custom engine but underestimates development time, the project may be cancelled and the team left without a product.

**Assumptions:** Studio "ToRa" is fictional. The $5M budget is fictional. The 3-year deadline is fictional. All scenario details are invented for this assignment.Unreal Engine and PlatinumEngine are real products; the reasons PlatinumGames chose a custom engine are documented in a 2020 interview (Gematsu, 2020-03-12).
